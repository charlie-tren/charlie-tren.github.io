Can People Stand Each Other?

What the Parties Disagree About

Each line is one thing parties argue about. It shows how far apart they are on that issue now, against how far apart they were in the 1970s. The dotted line marks the 1970s. Anything above it means the parties have drifted further apart since.

At each election, experts place every party on the issue. The line is how far apart those positions are, counting each party by the votes it won, divided by the same measure in the 1970s. Elections are skipped where the parties with a position took under 70% of the vote V-Party records.
