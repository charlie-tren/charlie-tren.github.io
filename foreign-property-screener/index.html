Notes

  • Growth is one number you set, applied to every country. The source workbook had a per-country estimate; most of it was guesswork, so none of it is used.
  • All cash. No Australian lender takes foreign property as security, so the leverage that makes property work at home is not on offer here.
  • The return does not depend on the purchase price. Rent, gain and costs all scale with it, so the price only sets the size of the numbers in the bars above, not the percentage. What the ranking does depend on is the rent, and that comes from Numbeo, which is user-contributed. No statistical agency publishes rental yields across countries, so there is no official figure to check it against.
  • General information, not advice.
