Notes

  • Ease of investing is six things a foreign buyer runs into, each scored from a stated fact: who may own, whether buying brings residency, whether money can leave, how long a sale takes, what buying costs, and the property-rights score. Open a row to see the six. It is not a recommendation, and the parts matter more than the total.
  • Yields come from Numbeo, which is user-contributed. No statistical agency publishes rental yields across countries, so there is nothing official to check them against. Treat them as indicative.
  • Prices are a median dwelling converted to Australian dollars, for scale rather than as a quote.
  • General information, not advice.
