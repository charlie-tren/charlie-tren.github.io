Notes on the Data

  • The rent figure is net of running costs and local property taxes, so holding costs are not deducted a second time.
  • House price growth is one number applied to every country. The source workbook carried a per-country growth estimate for all thirty-four; twenty-three of those were guesses with nothing behind them, so none of them are used here. Growth is yours to set.
  • Prices are a median dwelling, converted to Australian dollars. They are indicative rather than a quote on any particular property.
  • The purchase is all cash. No Australian lender takes foreign real property as security, and foreign lenders rarely write mortgages for non-resident foreigners, so the leverage that makes domestic property investment work is not on offer.
  • General information, not advice.
