Tools I've Built

LighthousePersonal project

A personal investment decision system - part analytical workbench, part decision journal.

  • Screening, DCF, reverse DCF and moat scoring across six asset classes.
  • Runs locally against a Bloomberg terminal, with every pull cached.
  • A decision journal holding the thesis, the trade, the outcome and the review.

VantagePersonal project

A Bloomberg-powered markets desktop app, shipped as an installable Windows program.

  • Eighteen modules across rates, FX, vol, macro and equities.
  • Curve shocks, vol surfaces, Monte-Carlo pricing, pairs backtesting, maturing debt.
  • Live Bloomberg terminal data behind every screen.

All-Weather AllocatorPersonal project

A regime-aware multi-asset portfolio allocator.

  • An inflation-and-rates regime model splitting history into four states, with a different allocation in each.
  • Twenty-six assets across five groups.
  • Ten objectives, from max Sharpe to hierarchical risk parity to minimising the worst drawdown.

PythonStreamlit

ParallaxPersonal project

A systematic momentum/trend system for equity CFDs.

  • Five scanners produce candidates; twenty-two kill-chain gates decide which get sized.
  • Valuation p-value from P/FCF, P/E, P/S and P/B, each ranked against a decade of the company’s own history.
  • 1,027 tests, most of them over the gates and position health.

BloombergTradingViewYahooPythonStreamlitSQLite

BTC AnalyserPersonal project

A Bitcoin accumulation model: how much to buy, and when not to.

  • Seven on-chain measures: MVRV-Z, Mayer multiple, drawdown, fear and greed, realised-price ratio, two-year moving average, Puell.
  • Backtested 2013 to 2026 against a straight dollar-cost average.
  • Leverage capped by liquidation distance.

Overlay & Hedging OpsInternal tool

An operations platform for running hedges across institutional mandates.

  • Custodian files, administrator feeds and monitored inboxes into one position store.
  • Daily revaluation of FX forwards and swaps, marked by counterparty.
  • Hedge ratios checked against each mandate's tolerance band, with the trade that corrects a breach.

Signal PipelinesInternal tool

Scheduled pipelines that turn public sources into a ranked daily digest.

  • Public filings and news, deduped against a persistent store.
  • Scored on relevance to an advisory book, into hot, warm and cool bands above hard exclusion gates.
  • Sector and market capitalisation added to every entity.
