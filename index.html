LighthousePersonal project

A personal investment decision system - part analytical workbench, part decision journal.

  • Screens and values a multi-asset universe - equities, macro, FX, crypto, super and CFDs - replacing an Excel workflow
  • Valuation lenses for DCF, fundamentals history, moat and quality, return-potential and drawdown
  • A buy-gate, kill-chain, sell-triggers and prohibitions govern each decision
  • A decision journal of theses, trades, outcomes and reviews, for backtesting the methodology behind them
  • Pure-Python core, a SQLite journal (alembic) with nightly backup, and a Bloomberg + Excel data layer

Bloomberg · Python · Streamlit · SQLite

ParallaxPersonal project

A systematic momentum/trend system for equity CFDs.

  • Breakout, compression, pullback and near-high screeners
  • A chart-quant score feeding a 14-gate risk "kill-chain"
  • Regime-aware position sizing, capped for correlation and concentration
  • Structural trailing stops with a break-even ratchet on exits
  • A point-in-time backtest with cost and friction modelling, plus a daily-review loop and decision log

Bloomberg · TradingView · Yahoo · Python · Streamlit · SQLite

BTC AnalyserPersonal project

A Bitcoin accumulation model - scores where BTC sits in its cycle and sizes contributions accordingly.

  • On-chain and cycle signals - MVRV-Z, Mayer multiple, Puell, realised-price ratio, 2-year MA and Fear & Greed
  • Blended into a composite score, then gated by a risk kill-chain
  • Leverage- and liquidation-aware contribution sizing
