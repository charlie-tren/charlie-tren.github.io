Inequality and the State

Every excavated site carries a coding for how the society that built it was organised, on Kohler's five-step scale. The wider a polity's reach, the more unevenly its houses were sized. One dot is one site; the bar is the median.

Two Hemispheres

Kohler's own headline finding, from the same sites. Eurasia had draught animals and the plough, which let a household work more land than it could farm by hand and let the surplus concentrate. The Americas did not.

What Each Layer Measures

Four kinds of evidence, each measuring a different unit over a different population. They barely overlap in time, so none of them validates the others.
