Notes on the Data

Colour
Blue is left, red is right, following American usage. Most of the world uses the reverse.
The sources disagree
Why it starts in 1975
The Database of Political Institutions, which supplies most of the series, starts in 1975. Coverage before that exists only for a largely Western set of countries, which would make the early decades a chart about Europe.
Dating
A country counts under the party in power on 1 January. A government formed in March counts from the next year.
