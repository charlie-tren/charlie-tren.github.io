Notes on the data

Two kinds of missing
Left–right does not apply is a finding rather than a gap. Saudi Arabia bans political parties; the governing party of the Philippines describes itself as a big tent spanning populism to self-declared socialism. No data records the absence of a usable source. The two are reported separately, and neither is removed by rescaling the remainder to 100%.
Colour convention
Blue denotes the left and red the right, following American usage. Most of the world uses the reverse.
Provisional years
The Database of Political Institutions ends in 2023. From 2024 the series relies on Wikidata and hand-coding, which together resolve about half the world's population, so those years are hatched. Movement across them reflects coverage rather than political change.
Dating convention
A country is recorded under the party of its chief executive on 1 January, following the Database of Political Institutions. A government formed in March is counted from the following year.
